ℹ️ What "Signal Floor (e.g. 2 of 5)" Means

The Signal Floor measures how many pitches in the arsenal exhibit physical mechanical separation that is statistically distinct from random delivery jitter (clearing ≥75% empirical discrimination). For example, "2 of 5" means 2 pitches in his 5-pitch mix (e.g., Fastball and Changeup) can be isolated with high physical certainty before release.
