Technical Architecture

Computer Vision & Deep Learning Stack

Built on production-grade computer vision models fine-tuned specifically on high-speed baseball pitching mechanics.

YOLOv8 Detection

High-Speed Object & Gear Localization

Custom-trained YOLOv8 models identify and segment critical equipment and anatomical regions with sub-pixel precision across changing broadcast lighting, jersey colors, and camera zooms:

  • Pitching Glove Bounding: Glove pocket orientation, flare angle, and set elevation.
  • Bare Hand Tracking: Hand depth, grip insertion timing, and finger curl exposure.
  • Catcher Setup Detection: Pre-pitch glove target placement and crouch width.
MediaPipe Pose

3D Skeletal Landmark Tracking

Full 33-point anatomical pose estimation maps full-body kinematics throughout the entire pre-pitch sequence:

  • Torso Lean & Spine Angle: Pre-pitch posture shifts correlated with off-speed pitches.
  • Lead Knee Lift Dynamics: Tempo acceleration and knee height variations.
  • Forearm Exposure: Forearm tilt angle relative to the pitcher's torso axis.
Strict Gating

Strict Temporal Gating (Zero Leakage)

To guarantee zero post-release data leakage and preserve true predictive utility:

  • Gated Window: Tracking begins when the pitcher settles into the set and terminates precisely at the frame of hand break.
  • No Statcast/Trajectory Leakage: Ball release velocity, trajectory, spin rate, and on-screen graphics are strictly excluded from feature computation.
