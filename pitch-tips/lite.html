Operational Workflow

Operational Workflow: From Automated Detection to On-Field Execution

How Major League clubs transform computer vision signals into on-field competitive advantage through a four-stage execution pipeline.

01 MODEL

The Model Finds the Pattern

Automated computer vision continuously scans multi-angle game video, tracking 30+ anatomical landmarks across uniform delivery strata to isolate and rank physical mechanical discrepancies before hand break.

02 ANALYSTS

Analysts Confirm the Tip

Advance scouts and quantitative analysts vet ranked leads against baseline pitch mixes, permutation null tests, and multi-game holdouts to confirm genuine statistical lift and rule out camera artifacts.

03 COACHES

Coaches Teach the Recognition

Hitting coaches, pitching coordinators, and advance staff distill validated biomechanical differences into intuitive visual triggers, situational recognition drills, and count-specific game plans.

04 PLAYERS

Players Recognize on the Field

Hitters in the batter's box, baserunners on the basepaths, and dugout staff execute live recognition before ball release—eliminating pitch options, sharpening swing decisions, and driving run production.
