Model Training & Fine-Tuning: Labeling trains the computer vision model to identify anatomical markers and equipment bounding boxes across varied camera angles. Fine-tuning requires diverse training samples per camera angle accounting for lighting variations (dark vs light / day vs night), different stadium backgrounds, jersey colors, player complexions, hand positioning, and glove angles to maximize detection accuracy.

Hand-in-Glove & Occluded Sets: Frames captured inside the actionable delivery window (set position through peak leg lift). Annotations train the detector to learn degrees of hand burial and exposure inside the glove based on camera angle. Box the visible portion of the hand even when only peeking out of or through the glove; label glove-only when no hand is visible at all. Both are required to fine-tune detection across stadium lighting, jersey colors, and hand positioning.
